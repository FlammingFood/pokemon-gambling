Pokémon Draft Royale — quick local multiplayer

Each round: players draft 4 Pokémon from a pool (pool size = players × 4). Then a random 12 Pokémon are rolled — any drafted Pokémon that appear are removed from that player's pool. Full removal = 3 points, 2nd most removed = 1 point. Next round pick order is from lowest → highest score. Good luck!
